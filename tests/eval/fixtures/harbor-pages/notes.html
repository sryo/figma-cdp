How reminders work

Automation Email Late fees Templates Net 30 Recurring invoices Multi-currency Partial payments

Harbor sends a friendly reminder three days before an invoice is due, another on the due date, and a firmer one each week after that until the balance is paid or you mark the invoice as settled by hand.

Every reminder uses the template of the customer's language and includes a link to pay online, the invoice as a PDF, and the outstanding amount after any partial payments have been taken off.

You can pause reminders for a single customer from their profile, or switch them off for the whole workspace while you review your payment terms with your accountant.
